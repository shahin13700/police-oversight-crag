## 4. Historical Context & Superseded Documents

Prior to April 1, 2024, police oversight in Ontario was governed by the *Police Services Act, R.S.O. 1990* through the Office of the Independent Police Review Director (OIPRD). With the proclamation of the CSPA on April 1, 2024, the OIPRD was reconstituted as LECA, and guidelines 001, 002, 003, and 005 were updated and reissued.

Earlier OIPRD versions (dated 2020–2021) have been removed from the active retrieval corpus to prevent the citation of repealed statutory authorities while preserving historical auditability.
